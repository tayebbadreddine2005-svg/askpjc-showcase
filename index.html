How it works

Retrieval first. Then the answer.

01 · ASK

Student asks

From the chat panel in the MyPJC portal, any time of day.

02 · FIND

Search official sources

Department categories route the question to the right official pages and documents, plus the live college calendar for dates.

03 · ANSWER

AI writes from sources

Claude Haiku or a local Qwen model answers only from what was found, with sources.

04 · CHECK

Safety checks

Crisis help comes first. Links not found in official sources are removed.

Inside the portal

Answers with sources.

Deadlines come straight from the college's live calendar feed, so dates like the last day to drop with a W update on their own each year. Every answer shows where it came from.

  • Hundreds of college web pages and official documents, organized by department
  • Office-confirmed answers take priority over older pages
  • Crisis questions get emergency resources first
  • Chat logs keep no passwords or personal data

The build

About two months.

Phase 1

Build the core

RAG app in Python (FastAPI and BM25 search) with Claude Haiku, embedded in the MyPJC portal. Crisis detection, link allowlist, rate limits, privacy-safe logs and a staff review page.

Phase 2

Build the knowledge base

Read every college web page and document. Departments confirmed answers. Organized everything by department and added the live calendar feed.

Phase 3

Set up our own AI server

NVIDIA GB10 machine running open-source Qwen models with llama.cpp, so student questions can stay on college hardware.

Phase 4

Test at scale

21,000+ test questions across Claude Haiku and open-source Qwen models to compare accuracy, speed and cost before choosing what students see.

Lessons

Good data. Good answers.

The model matters. Checking every fact with the people who own it matters more.

  • Office-confirmed answers always win over older pages
  • Organize content by department before scaling up
  • Test thousands of real questions before trusting a model
  • Never let the AI invent a link or a date
